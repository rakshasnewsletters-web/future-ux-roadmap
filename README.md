# AI-Ready UX Careers

Workshop - UX Design in the Age of AI- Rough Structure

Hosted by Charul (Founder, Louve Studio) & Rakshita

1. Why This Workshop

The core motto is to spread real awareness about design — for the upcoming generation entering the field, and for people who currently feel anxious that AI will take over design jobs. Alongside that, we want to help attendees understand how to actually build a strong career in UX design in this new landscape.

We want people to walk away from this session having genuinely learned or gained something concrete — not just sat through a talk.

2. Logistics

Detail Info Proposed Date Saturday, October 3rd (next Saturday) Duration 1.5 hours total — 1 hour session + 30 min Q&A Platform Zoom/ G-meet Format Live, interactive session Topic UX Design in the Age of AI Sub-Topics

3. Topic & Audience

Main Topic: UX Design in the Age of AI — Portfolio & Big Startup/MNC Hiring Strategy

Target Audience: Beginners to mid-level UX designers, and design leads preparing for roles at large startups/MNCs

4. Rough Session Structure (1 hour + 30 min Q&A)

Section 1 — UX Design in the Age of AI (~15–20 min)

How the role of a UX designer is evolving with AI tools; what's changing, what's not, and how to stay relevant/irreplaceable rather than fear displacement.

Section 2 — How to Get Hired at Big Startups/Companies (~15–20 min)

What top companies (like Razorpay, Stripe-level orgs) actually look for in a UX hire today; hiring process insights, common mistakes, standing out.

Section 3 — Portfolio Strategy (~15–20 min)

How to build/structure a portfolio that reflects AI-era relevance and gets noticed by senior hiring managers.

Section 4 — Live Q&A (30 min)

Open floor for attendees to ask direct questions.

5. What We Want Attendees to Walk Away With

A clear, non-fearful understanding of where design is headed with AI

Actionable hiring insights from someone who's actually been on the inside (IIT Roorkee → Razorpay → Stripe)

A real framework for improving their portfolio, not just generic tips
